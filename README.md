
# 📊 Regional Business Review | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on creating a one-page interactive Power BI dashboard for a company's **Regional Business Review**.

The goal is to provide the executive team with a consolidated overview of business performance, highlighting total revenue, order volume, regional sales performance, product category sales, and revenue contribution by category.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- 💰 Track total revenue generated during Q4 2024.
- 🛒 Monitor the total number of orders.
- 🌎 Compare sales performance across different regions.
- 📦 Analyze units sold by product category.
- 📈 Evaluate each product category's contribution to total revenue.
- 📊 Present key business metrics in a centralized executive dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights key business metrics:

- **Total Revenue:** Overall revenue generated during the reporting period.
- **Total Orders:** Total number of orders processed.

### 2. Sales by Region

A visual comparison of sales revenue across different regions.

This helps identify:
- Highest-performing regions.
- Regional revenue differences.
- Potential opportunities for business growth.

### 3. Units Sold by Category

A category-level breakdown of the total quantity of products sold.

This helps evaluate product demand and identify categories with higher sales volumes.

### 4. Revenue Contribution by Category

A pie chart displaying the proportion of total revenue contributed by each product category.

This helps the executive team understand how individual categories contribute to overall business revenue.

This dashboard provides executives with a centralized view of regional business performance.
